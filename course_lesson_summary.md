# Course, Lesson, and Practice Summary

## 📘 Course: Speakout Starter (A1)

### Lesson 1: Greetings & Introductions
**🎧 Listening Practice:** Dengarkan dialog. Ulangi 2 kali. Pause, lalu coba ucapkan sendiri.
**🗣️ Speaking Practice:** Hello, my name is [nama kamu]. Nice to meet you.

### Lesson 2: Numbers & Countries
**🎧 Listening Practice:** Dengarkan orang menyebutkan angka dan negara.
**🗣️ Speaking Practice:** I'm from [your country]. Where are you from?

### Lesson 3: Family & Friends
**🎧 Listening Practice:** Dengarkan perkenalan keluarga.
**🗣️ Speaking Practice:** This is my [family member].

### Lesson 4: Daily Routines
**🎧 Listening Practice:** Dengarkan rutinitas harian orang lain.
**🗣️ Speaking Practice:** I wake up at ___.

### Lesson 5: Food & Drinks
**🎧 Listening Practice:** Dengarkan orang menyebut makanan favorit.
**🗣️ Speaking Practice:** I like [food]. I don't like [food].

### Lesson 6: Time & Days
**🎧 Listening Practice:** Dengarkan pengumuman waktu.
**🗣️ Speaking Practice:** It's __ o'clock.

### Lesson 7: Hobbies & Free Time
**🎧 Listening Practice:** Dengarkan orang bicara tentang hobinya.
**🗣️ Speaking Practice:** I like [verb-ing].

### Lesson 8: Describing People
**🎧 Listening Practice:** Dengarkan deskripsi orang.
**🗣️ Speaking Practice:** My friend is ___.

### Lesson 9: Shopping
**🎧 Listening Practice:** Dengarkan percakapan di toko.
**🗣️ Speaking Practice:** How much is this?

### Lesson 10: Around Town / Directions
**🎧 Listening Practice:** Dengarkan instruksi arah.
**🗣️ Speaking Practice:** Where is the __?

### Lesson 11: Weather & Seasons
**🎧 Listening Practice:** Dengarkan ramalan cuaca.
**🗣️ Speaking Practice:** It's [weather] today.

### Lesson 12: Review & Consolidation
**🎧 Listening Practice:** Dengarkan percakapan gabungan (perkenalan, hobi, belanja).
**🗣️ Speaking Practice:** Simulasi percakapan sehari-hari.

## 📘 Course: Speakout Advanced (C1)

### Lesson 1: Advanced Communication
**🎧 Listening Practice:** Dengarkan debat formal vs casual chat.
**🗣️ Speaking Practice:** Break the ice with your partner. Use inversion: "Never have I felt so …"

### Lesson 2: Global Issues
**🎧 Listening Practice:** Dengarkan wawancara aktivis lingkungan.
**🗣️ Speaking Practice:** It could be argued that …. One possible solution is ….

### Lesson 3: Advanced Storytelling
**🎧 Listening Practice:** Dengarkan cerita pribadi inspiratif.
**🗣️ Speaking Practice:** Tell your biggest turning point story.

### Lesson 4: Academic Writing Skills
**🎧 Listening Practice:** Dengarkan kuliah singkat tentang essay structure.
**🗣️ Speaking Practice:** Present a mini-argument using linking words.

### Lesson 5: Business & Economics

**🗣️ Speaking Practice:** By 2030, I will have been ___.

### Lesson 6: Media & Society

**🗣️ Speaking Practice:** What bothers me most is ….

### Lesson 7: Innovation & Ethics

### Lesson 8: Culture & Identity

### Lesson 9: Science & Society

### Lesson 10: Philosophy & Critical Thinking

### Lesson 11: Advanced Negotiation
